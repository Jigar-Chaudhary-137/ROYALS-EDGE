2. ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS OF PRELIMINARY DISCREPANCIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Issue 1: Divergence between static markdown summaries and analytical data
  - Finding: Early exploratory prototypes utilized static summary strings containing
    draft heuristic figures (e.g., Rinku Singh 96.9, Tim David 66.5, Rashid Khan 70.2).
    Subsequent algorithmic refinements (incorporating boundary rate weights and Bayesian
    prior adjustments) updated the DataFrames, but static display texts in the frontend
    export component and initial PDF drafts retained preliminary figures.
  - Correction: Implemented a strict Single Source of Truth architecture. The PDF
    generator (`backend/pdf_generator.py`) and export summary (`frontend/src/pages/ExportCentre.jsx`)
    now dynamically render directly from the verified DataFrames and CSV files.

Issue 2: Batting Average Discrepancy (e.g., Rinku Singh 43.3 vs 125.67)
  - Finding: Initial code computed batting average as `runs / matches` rather than the
    cricket definition of `runs / dismissals`. Because Rinku Singh was dismissed only 3
    times across his 13 pressure innings (scoring 377 runs with 10 not-outs), dividing
    by total innings artificially deflated his average to ~29-43.
  - Correction: Corrected to official ICC batting average definition:
    Pressure Average = Pressure Runs / max(1, Pressure Dismissals).
    Rinku Singh's verified high-pressure average is exactly 377 / 3 = 125.67.

Issue 3: Bowler Overs and Legal Balls (e.g., Rashid Khan 41.3 vs 39.2 overs)
  - Finding: Earlier scripts converted deliveries by dividing total balls (including
    wides and no-balls) by 6.0, conflating illegal deliveries with legal balls.
  - Correction: Strictly implemented ICC Law 21 & 22: Bowler legal deliveries count
    only when `is_bowler_legal == True` (excluding wides and no-balls).
    Rashid Khan bowled 249 legal balls = 41 completed overs + 3 legal balls (41.3 overs).
    Mustafizur Rahman bowled 231 legal balls = 38 completed overs + 3 balls (38.3 overs).
    Economic rates are computed as: Runs Conceded / (Legal Balls / 6.0).

Issue 4: Team and Competition Context Mapping
  - Finding: Non-IPL competitions were occasionally listed in franchise columns, or
    players were assigned ambiguous team affiliations.
  - Correction: Created `get_player_team_context()` in `backend/analytics.py` which
    strictly distinguishes:
    a) IPL Franchise: Player's most recent registered IPL team (or "Non-IPL").
    b) National Team: Player's international country representation.
    c) Display Label: Formatted as `IPL Franchise / National Team` for IPL players,
       and `Country (International)` for international players without recent IPL contracts.

3. VERIFIED CRICKET CALCULATION RULES & METHODOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Match Pressure Index (MPI in [0, 100]):
   - Vectorized delivery-level algorithm accounting for:
     1. Required Run Rate (RRR) vs Par Economy in 2nd innings chases.
     2. Wickets in hand leverage factor.
     3. Innings stage leverage (Death Overs 16-20 par pressure).
     4. Match closeness (win margin <= 15 runs or <= 2 wickets).
     5. 1st Innings collapse penalty (>= 3 wickets lost inside powerplay).
     6. Super Over leverage (MPI = 100.0) and Playoff/Knockout bonus (+8.0).
   - High-Pressure Threshold: MPI >= 70.0.
   - Total High-Pressure Deliveries Analyzed: Exactly 18,414 balls (9.16% of dataset).

B. Batting Pressure Rating (BPR in [0, 100]):
   - Empirical Bayes Adjusted Strike Rate:
     Adj_SR = (Pressure_Balls * Pressure_SR + 40 * Career_SR) / (Pressure_Balls + 40)
   - Empirical Bayes Adjusted Average:
     Adj_Avg = (Pressure_Balls * Pressure_Avg + 40 * Career_Avg) / (Pressure_Balls + 40)
   - Normalized Weights:
     - Adjusted Strike Rate: 28%
     - Adjusted Batting Average: 22%
     - Strike Rate Acceleration (Pressure SR - Career SR): 20%
     - Boundary Scoring % (4s & 6s / Balls): 15%
     - Dot Ball Minimization % (100 - Dot%): 15%
   - Sample Size Confidence Multiplier:
     Confidence = clip(Pressure_Balls / 140.0, 0.75, 1.0)
     Final BPR = Raw_Composite * Confidence

C. Bowling Pressure Rating (BPR in [0, 100]):
   - Empirical Bayes Adjusted Economy:
     Adj_Econ = (Legal_Balls * Pressure_Econ + 36 * Career_Econ) / (Legal_Balls + 36)
   - Empirical Bayes Adjusted Wicket Strike Rate:
     Adj_Wkt_SR = (Legal_Balls * Pressure_SR + 36 * Career_SR) / (Legal_Balls + 36)
   - Normalized Weights:
     - Adjusted Economy Rate (Lower is better): 30%
     - Economy Improvement vs Baseline (Career Econ - Pressure Econ): 20%
     - Adjusted Wicket Strike Rate (Balls per Wicket): 20%
     - Dot Ball Percentage (Pressure Dots / Legal Balls): 18%
     - Boundary Concession Minimization: 12%
   - Sample Size Confidence Multiplier:
     Confidence = clip(Legal_Balls / 150.0, 0.75, 1.0)
     Final Bowling Rating = Raw_Composite * Confidence
